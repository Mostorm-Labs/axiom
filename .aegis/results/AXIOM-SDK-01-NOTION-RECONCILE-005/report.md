# SDK-01 Notion boundary reconciliation — read-only finding

During follow-up, Notion search found [Axiom Public Runtime Facade v0.2](https://app.notion.com/p/3c84c57a590c81b7a5d8d15997502eaf) and [ABI / Bridge Matrix v0.1](https://app.notion.com/p/3c44c57a590c81b3b3a0ddc38cf3b158). These are source pages, not an SDK-01 P31/P32 acceptance. The connector reports both pages' verification metadata as `unverified`, even though their page content respectively calls itself Current Public Semantic Authority and Current Direction. Their reported last edits were 2026-08-26; neither was altered in this task.

The Facade page distinguishes Product Shell control/presentation from Shared Data Runtime's AxiomDataBridge persistence/replay/operation stream, and describes `AxiomRuntime -> AxiomCanvas [0..N]` with at most one attached Document per Canvas. It requires non-reentrant coarse public events and separate command-accepted, state-applied and frame-presented stages. The SDK-01 candidate's one-active-View limit is a proposed first-version supported subset, not evidence that the underlying public semantic model permits only one Canvas; preserve explicit target identity and fail clearly on unsupported extra views. Do not collapse persistence/sync into Facade or reclassify submitted frames as presented.

The ABI matrix identifies the ICF-01 common DataBridge C ABI as Proposed Freeze Candidate and platform realization separately; exact public bindings, presentation descriptors, IME methods and some threading remain open. The accepted V1 C contract's synchronous callback transport and the Facade page's coarse asynchronous product events apply at potentially different layers. Do not declare these contradictory or equivalent until the SDK bridge defines and verifies the mapping.

The SDK-01 v0.2 draft package predates this read-only reconciliation and does not lock these Notion page bytes. A subsequent accepted package must bind exact authoritative source revisions/content and resolve their relationship to V1 callbacks and the one-View first-version restriction. This observation does not approve P32 or change the already recorded user decision on stale-request rejection and explicit retry.
